Wasted Clips Bot

The private tool the Wasted Clips YouTube channel uses to publish its own GTA 6 commentary Shorts.

What it does

  • Uploads short commentary videos, made by the channel owner, to the Wasted Clips channel.
  • Changes the privacy setting of videos it posted (for example, making a video private).
  • Reads view counts and YouTube Analytics for that channel, to learn which videos work best.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Wasted Clips channel. It is not offered to the public, and it never accesses anyone else's YouTube account.
