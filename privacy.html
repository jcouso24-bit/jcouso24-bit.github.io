Privacy Policy

Wasted Clips Bot · Effective November 18, 2026

Wasted Clips Bot is a private tool used only by the owner of the Wasted Clips YouTube channel to publish that channel's own videos. This policy explains what the app can access and how that information is handled.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and approves access, the app can:

  • upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel;
  • view and manage videos on that channel, such as setting a video it posted to private;
  • view YouTube Analytics reports for that channel, such as views by country.

The app does not request or read email, contacts, files or any other Google data, and it never accesses anyone else's account.

How the information is used

Access is used only to post the channel's own videos, manage their privacy settings, and read the channel's view statistics to see which videos perform best. Nothing is used for advertising.

Storage and sharing

  • The sign-in key from Google is stored only on the channel owner's own computer.
  • No YouTube or Google data is sold, shared with, or sent to anyone else.
  • View statistics are kept on the same computer for the owner's own use.

Google API Services

Wasted Clips Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored key can also be deleted from the owner's computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
